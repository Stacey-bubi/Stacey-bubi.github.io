## Why binary contrasts were never enough

The [earlier formalisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03658) defines a concept direction from
counterfactual pairs: queen minus king, woman minus man. It is precise and it works, but it needs
the concept to have a natural opposite.

Most concepts do not. There is no natural counterfactual for "bird". You can contrast it with
"mammal", but that is a choice among several, and different choices give different directions. The
formalism had nothing to say about the majority of what a model knows.

## Polytopes instead of directions

The fix is to stop asking for a direction and start asking for a shape.

A categorical concept with several mutually exclusive values is represented as a **polytope** —
for $k$ values, a simplex — with each value at a vertex. A binary concept is then the degenerate
case: two vertices, and the line between them is the familiar direction.

Then the hierarchical result. Semantic containment between concepts corresponds to a specific
geometric relationship between their representations. The taxonomy is not merely metadata about
the concepts; it is present in the arrangement of the vectors.

This is worth pausing on. The model was trained to predict the next token. Nothing in that
objective requires the WordNet hierarchy to be recoverable from the geometry of its vocabulary
space, and yet it is.

## The pattern I keep noticing

Two papers on this list, from unrelated directions, both replace a line with a region.

Here, a categorical concept is a simplex rather than a direction. In
[Concept Cones](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.17420), refusal is a cone rather than a direction. The
first is a statement about how meaning is organised, the second about how behaviour is mediated,
and they were not written in conversation with each other.

If a concept generally occupies a region, then any method whose whole plan is to find *the*
direction and remove it is working with the wrong shape from the start. Not fatal — removing a line
from a region still does something — but it explains why single directions keep turning out to be
incomplete.

## Its limits

It shares the parent paper's main gap: this is the representation space at the edges of the model,
not the intermediate layers where steering and ablation happen, so the connection to interventions
is not made.

The concepts are also WordNet nouns — clean, well-structured and hand-curated. Whether messier
concepts like refusal or harmfulness have this structure is an open question, and those are the
ones I care about. And with two model families tested, transfer is not guaranteed: as the earlier
paper showed with LLaMA-2 versus Gemma, geometric findings do not always survive a change of model.
